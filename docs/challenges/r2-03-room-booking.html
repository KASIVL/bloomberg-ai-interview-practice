ENGINEERING TICKET · ROUND 02

Study Room Booking

TICKET #03
OPENBUGPYTHON

Ticket: Students are double-booked in Cappy’s study rooms

Problem

Two complaints came in this week. A group booked a room from 9:30 to 11:30 and walked in on another group that already had 10:00 to 11:00. Separately, a student cancelled a booking, but nobody could book that time afterward. Investigate the booking flow across the routes, booking service, repository, and model.

Acceptance criteria

  • A room can never hold two active bookings whose times overlap, whatever the shape of the overlap.
  • Back-to-back bookings (one ends at 11:00, the next starts at 11:00) are allowed.
  • A cancelled booking no longer blocks its time slot.
  • Only the student who made a booking can cancel it.
  • Bookings are between 08:00 and 22:00 and last at most two hours; invalid requests fail without changing state.
  • Bookings are stored in the included in-memory repository; no database is needed.

Investigation

Draw the existing booking and the new one on a timeline, then list every way two time ranges can relate. Check which of those cases the overlap check and the tests actually cover. Read each test name and ask whether its assertion proves what the name claims. Make a focused fix and add regression tests for the cases you verify.

Follow-up: system design

Once your tests pass, talk this through with a partner: the booking service now runs on three servers behind a load balancer, and 5,000 students try to book rooms at 8:00 AM on the first day of finals week. What breaks first, and how would you change the design so a room still can’t be double-booked?
